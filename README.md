## Editing the profile

- Images in `profile/README.md` must use absolute URLs of the form `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/IBN-n-Drop-Table/.github/main/<path>`. Relative paths do not work on an organization profile.
- Keep images under about 500 KB. For larger ones, add an optimized copy (PNG or WebP, at most 1600 px wide) to `assets/` and keep the original.
- Captions say which telescope or survey each image comes from (SPHEREx, DSS2, WISE) and describe only what the screenshot shows. Nothing is simulated and nothing is called a discovery.
- Changes pushed to `main` appear on the organization page right away.
